Installation sequence

  1. Confirm the supplied assembly. Match quote Rev B to the generic Rev C drawing, with actual mass, centre of gravity, mounting feet and cooling equipment.
  2. Finalize the common skid. Resolve the rear cantilever and pump housing support together; keep the engine and pump aligned on the same base.
  3. Match the drive interface. Confirm the configured pump, rotation, coupling, spline engagement and power/torque control before drilling.
  4. Finish services. Locate the remote fuel filters, exhaust shielding, hydraulic lines and fan guarding. Obtain an approved longer control harness for the cab.
  5. Complete installed checks. Verify hot-weather cooling, service removal, movement clearances, braking and structural details before release.

Two supplier questions affect the design. The quote’s horsepower description conflicts with Kubota’s continuous rating, and unrestricted engine peak torque exceeds the candidate 13-tooth pump shaft’s catalog maximum. LT25 uses provisional power/torque limits; no limiter or complete pump package has been selected or validated.

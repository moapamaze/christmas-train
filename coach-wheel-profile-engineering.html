Wheel / track definition

Project record PF-3R2-R0 does not select a rail or turnout. Its 30ASCE paper reference is 79.375 mm high and wide at the base, while the linked CAD face envelope is 88.9×88.9 mm. The CAD was quarantined rather than scaled. The nominal 36 in gauge alone cannot set flange thickness, back-to-back, flangeway or check gauge.

The current wheel blank has inboard rim faces Y±430, hence 860 mm between blank backs. Hub centres are Y±480, hence 960 mm. Neither is a released finished-wheel dimension. The Ø640 flange blank above a Ø600 running reference provides 20 mm radial excess, not an approved flange height. The profile drawing deliberately contains no invented finished coordinate table.

ARISO/RISSB’s profile-development guidance treats wheel/rail assessment, testing and implementation as a coordinated process. APTA’s flange-angle document provides useful reference concepts but its own scope excludes tourist/scenic/excursion and isolated-installation operations; no APTA profile or 72° rule has been adopted automatically for this project.

Gross wheel-load screen

Current upper coach allocations are 13,200 kg regular and 11,000 kg accessible. Equal division over 8 wheels gives 16.181 and 13.484 kN. This gross-load proxy does not resolve unsprung weight or passenger imbalance. Bearing centres Y±620 and contact proxies Y±480 give 140 mm overhang. For the regular coach, nominal maximum M=2.265 kNm; bending stress is 23.07 MPa at Ø100 and 17.34 MPa at Ø110. At the Ø90 journal entrance Y600, the 20 mm arm gives 4.52 MPa, not the full 140 mm overhang result.

Signed brake reactions: why the simple screen is incomplete

C5.4/C5.5 carrier equations allow a nominal 6 kNm equilibrium reconstruction. At 120 mm effective pad radius, forces on the axle at Y85 are[−6.667,0,+36.111] kN and at Y175[−23.333,0,−86.111] kN. The rotor applies[0,0,+50] kN at friction plane Y240 and −6 kNm about the axle. To represent transfer through the actual hub, the model places that force at assumed Y340 with an equivalent −5 kNm X couple. Forces and couples reverse for reverse braking.

Regular 13.2 t gross vertical loads, nominal suspension pose, zero additional brake-mass probe, quasi-static support reactions and two rail-torque splits produce:

All model force and moment residuals close numerically. The concentrated hub-transfer station produces an artificial moment jump; actual hub contact, split-screw preload and key engagement require a distributed joint model. These are unnotched gross-section values, not key-root stress, allowable load or fatigue results. The 6 kNm equilibrium needs wheel tangential/vertical ratios 0.618 for 50/50 or 1.236 at the single wheel for 0/100: structural hypotheses, not accepted adhesion or braking performance. Unknown actual brake output, inertia, load transfer, lateral loads and material allowables remain material gaps.

Press-fit sensitivity — no interference selected

A solid Ø110 shaft in a simplified Ø200×140 cylindrical hub, both elastic steel with E 210 GPa and ν0.30, gives p=665.795×δd MPa for diametral interference δd in mm. Nominal contact area is 48,380.5 mm². Axial slip estimate=μpA; torque slip estimate=μpA×0.055 m. Combined demand must use √(Faxial²+(T/0.055)²), not both independent slip maxima together.

Bearing fits, spacer and seal findings

SKF’s rotating-inner-ring roller-bearing guidance at this diameter distinguishes m6/n6/p6 according to load and peaks; no class is released here. Catalog surface/form guidance includes Ra 0.8 μm and runout controls; residual internal clearance must be checked with the actual interference and temperature. Ø160 H7 is a review candidate for a stationary outer-ring load with sliding freedom, subject to SKF confirmation.

TheØ104 shoulder exceeds the catalog 101 minimum; adjacent fillet radius must not exceed 2, but the exact radius and chamfer clearance remain held. The existing 3 mm spacer bore Ø90.2 surrounds a Ø86 relief: 2.1 mm radial freedom and no positive pilot. Its centering, clamped-face overlap and the square CAD relief/groove junction require a revised approved detail.

The existing Ø107 opening around Ø104 is 1.5 mm radial clearance. SKF 100 VA R is a possible locating-side face-seal candidate: its shaft range includes 104, but its installed-width allowance 9±1.2 mm does not cover the floating end’s ±3 travel. The 107 opening is already its nominal maximum, leaving no tolerance/eccentricity margin. It has not been installed or selected. A radial seal with a compatible land/gland, or verified labyrinth, must address floating movement, tilt, contamination and grease retention together.

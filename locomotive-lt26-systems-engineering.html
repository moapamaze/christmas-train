Cooling and support decisions

Proposed flow: paired side inlet → cold compartment → hydraulic cooler → removable transition → package radiator/CAC region → existing suction fan → engine bay → aft outlets. The nominal gross projected inlet area is 0.650 m² and outlet area 0.390 m². Neither is effective free area; louver blades, screens, inlet turn, cores, shroud and exits all require restriction data. No cooling capacity or opening-area ratio is accepted.

The cold plenum has a forward partition and removable floor at Z1760. Edge seals, drains and fasteners remain provisional. The cold bulkhead is body-supported with a flexible cooler seal; duct weight must not be carried by the radiator. The transition needs an independent bracket and flexible ends. This is an engineering proposal informed by Cummins T-030, ventilation/duct guidance pp201–202 and212, not approval of the Kubota/AIE package. AIE must approve its configured fan, core stack, restriction and hot-weather performance.

The new X4650 support adds a candidate load path from the aft common skid to the frame. Its two posts meet the current frame top and a lowered crossbeam; isolator spaces remain between that beam and the existing skid rails. The pump-support carrier has no assumed OEM bolt pattern or housing clamp. The extra support must be checked for load sharing and alignment rather than simply added as a rigid extra constraint.

Danfoss H1 Basic Information, pp40–44 treats overhung moment, coupling-induced radial loads and supplemental supports. Actual pump mass/CG, vibration, coupling and AIE housing limits remain necessary. This overlay does not approve any supplier flange load.

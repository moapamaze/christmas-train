CAB C2 / ENGINEERING RECORD

What fits, what changed, what remains open.

Cab C2 is integrated with the retained LT32 locomotive and first-coach connection. The earlier C1 and LT32 packages remain available as historical baselines. One coach is shown in the combined model; it is therefore the last coach and carries the tail camera. Additional coaches repeat the protected trunk and disconnect module.

Camera coverage and cleaning

Each camera uses a declared lens datum and an ideal rectangular field of 109° horizontal × 85° vertical. Front lens [300,0,1800] in locomotive coordinates; rear lens [9100,350,2200] in last-coach coordinates. Both aim 20° down, with 10° and 30° comparison cases. The exterior rectangular bodies are simplified envelopes, not OEM CAD. Exact lens offsets and aim calibration remain supplier inputs.

The 12,078 ground samples combine actual opaque mesh triangle occlusion with the declared field. At 20° down, the ideal near-ground boundary is about 0.94 m ahead of the front lens and 1.15 m beyond the rear lens. The 0.5 m sample grid is coarser than those boundaries. The plot extends 30 m; that is a plotting choice, not a detection range. Regions under the cameras, the train’s flanks, couplings and intermediate coaches remain blind. Lens distortion, image resolution, rain, dirt, glare, darkness and motion are not validated.

Camera guards have no bar ahead of the lens plane. Cleaning envelopes show level service-ground access at the locomotive and a standing/hand region on the retained rear coach platform. The latter requires an occupied reach check around the hood without standing on the coupling or passenger guardrail. Mount adapters, protective guards and frame attachments remain unrated.

The lower minimum wheel-load result is a material change for the next running-gear review. The model retains the LT23 spring preloads and the same six mass envelopes, duty cases and signed 50 m curve assumptions. It does not establish wheel/rail safety, adhesion, brake capacity, suspension performance or accepted speed.

The ledger replaces the 150 kg CAB-FITOUT reserve, both sidewalls, cab front/floor, eight former steps, old cab/rear BP/FP routes and clamps, the roof row and crew row. Solid parts use actual CAD volume and centroid with declared trial density. Seats, electronics, trim and cable/tube corridors use explicit allowances. The roof mass is relocated upward 120 mm rather than duplicated. Two 100 kg occupants are assigned to their revised seated CGs. The 350 kg BODY-MISSING residual reserve remains, with possible overlap explicitly retained because it has no defensible itemized allocation. Travel cases use stowed ladders and nominal seats.

Revised brake-line continuity

BP runs from[8300,−760,1740] through the C1-style underfloor path and new rear extension to[11140,−520,1100]. FP runs from[8300,−820,1740] to[11140,+520,1100]. The retained LT32 supported turning/vertical jumper interfaces remain. The obsolete above-floor cab lines, rear extensions and drop clamps are removed from the C2 assembly and mass ledger. New rear extensions are separated longitudinally atX11180 and 11280; geometric bends are 30 mm centreline radius, with 75 mm underfloor bends. Actual tube, pressure, fittings, minimum bends, drainability, support load, motion and brake-system acceptance remain open.

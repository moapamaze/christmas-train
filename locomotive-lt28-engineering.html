LT28 · ENGINEERING REPORT

Coordinated geometry, conditional margins.

This revision integrates the LT27 ±14 mm slide candidate with the installation supplement and reruns the reduced load model. It provides calculation evidence and connection sections for review; it does not release dimensions, components, manufacturing or operation.

Pin, sleeve, collar and weld demand

Normal radial demand is a gross body-reaction proxy before roll-cartridge sharing. The pin is screened as a simply supported Ø90 beam over 155 mm cheek-centre spacing, loaded at the sleeve centre. This envelopes uniform 70 mm sleeve loading under the same ideal beam assumptions; local contact, fit, notches, retention and fatigue remain open.

Emergency axial catch

One collar carries the catch in each direction. The assumed load path is ring → seated collar → cheek → crosshead → frame. A failed-link sensitivity applies the maximum normal link-force proxy (22.75 kN) through as much as 20.586 mm free travel. The additional 4 mm contributes 91.0 J before contact. Actual relative speed, effective mass, stiffness, energy absorption and post-failure alignment remain unknown.

The table is an elastic sensitivity at zero initial relative speed, not a predicted surviving impact. A finite speed adds kinetic energy. Material yielding, ring edge loading, collar local bending, cheek bending, weld tearing and frame load paths require a nonlinear impact/structural assessment.

The weld screen assumes a continuous 6 mm OD109 fillet, throat 0.707×leg, loaded as a retention/failover ring. In a properly seated joint, axial compression primarily bears directly into the cheek.

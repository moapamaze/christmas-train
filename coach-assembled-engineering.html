Physical changes made

The bridge ends now use a full 12 mm closure and a stepped 20 mm endplate and an86.5 mm-high closure. The endplate top is Z758, placing the 6 mm girder weld below the rounded HSS corner. The corner screen allows an outside radius up to19.05 mm (3×6.35), giving4.45 mm nominal clearance above the weld toe atZ764; confirm the actual supplied tube. Two 10 mm horizontal welds connect closure to endplate. The box remains 254 ×76.2 with 10 mm walls. Actual weld envelopes are included in the revised CAD and upper-frame clearance test.

The central bogie chord is lowered 12 mm, to Z438, with the closing plate at Z426–438. Upper shoulders rejoin at X±350. Outer spring-seat ribs move clear of the capture hardware, taper from 40 to 8 mm high over 128 mm, and use a Ø90 outer capture washer. Keeper bolt heads now seat at the plate underside, and the guide columns have actual blind tapped-hole envelopes.

Rubber contact moves from 35 to 25 mm jounce. Integral metal shoulders limit nominal travel to32 mm. Two 12 ×140 ×50 ribs above each stop transfer load from the 16 mm guide bridge into the side webs; the unreinforced plate did not pass the conservative strip screen. Block/shoulder solids are separated in the viewer for inspection; integral machining is the preferred metal-stop construction.

At 200 kN and bar yaw 7° / pitch 3°, the body-A force components are about 198.2 kN longitudinal, 24.3 kN lateral and 10.5 kN vertical; pushing reverses all three. Independent secondary connection

Two separate girder-mounted anchor proposals avoid using the primary pin, ears, backplate or crosshead. The 180 × 200 × 20 doubler has a 70 × 170 upper notch to clear the existing end crossmember, leaving a 50 mm bottom web. It and the 140 × 100 × 30 lug are preliminary; the lug has a Ø44 interface hole. Anchors are at local X−70, Y±722.8, Z700. This position raises the slack route clear of the main drawbar. Local diaphragms, welds and the frame's one-sided catch load still need design.

Across the sampled poses, anchor chords range from 747.3 to 1135.1 mm. The 1200 mm route retains at least 64.9 mm geometric excess over the longest chord. Its 90 mm envelope stays at least 166.6 mm above the local flat rail datum. This excludes real track elevations, fittings, chain dynamics and obstacles.

The orange tubes are 90 mm outside route envelopes with 1200 mm flexible pin-to-pin length, including reserved end fittings. They use a parabolic slack approximation. They are not actual chain links, a chain cut length, shackle CAD or an approved safety connection. Chain assemblies transmit tension only; they do not replace the main drawbar for pushing.

Identifiable sourcing leads are Peerless 26 mm Grade 100 chain, raw-chain stock 5511143 (59,700 lb WLL), and Crosby G-2140 1½ in, stock 1021110 (30 tonne WLL, Ø42 pin). The 36.1 mm chain inside width does not accept that pin directly: a supplier-matched enlarged end link is required. Peerless prohibits shock loading; these lifting ratings do not approve a slack catch. No purchase or complete restraint selection has been made.

A single side catching 200 kN at Y722.8 introduces 144.6 kN·m yaw moment before angled forces. Do not assume equal sharing. With the old mass pair, 1 m/s relative separation represents about 5.64 kJ; an assumed 50 mm arrest stroke would require 112.7 kN average arrest force, with peak and ongoing train forces additional. That stroke is not an available chain extension. Capture energy and manufacturer acceptance block finalization.

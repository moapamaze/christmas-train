Evidence and geometry

1,811 stored or mapped case records give 3,622 front/rear slide observations. This comprises 504 LT26 poses, 294 LT23, 306 LT24 and 168 LT25 archived equilibria mapped to 30 m positive-hand geometry, 504 concurrent LT26 installation-supplement poses, plus 35 retained LT23 geometry poses. The supplement uses signed 30 m reference loads and excludes the added fixture masses. Historical cases include sensitivity studies and are not independent operating validations.

Overall displacement spans −4.401 to +6.586 mm. The wider archived governing pose is LT23 geometry case31, front truck. The current 50 m governing case remains front-heavy / all coaches unbraked / 0.7 m/s² demand / opposed twist B / left turn: +6.225 mm. With ±14 mm stops it has 7.775 mm raw gap and 3.775 mm after the assumed allowance.

Candidate stop faces lie at ±49 mm from the pivot, outside the70 mm ring. The cheek inner faces remain±65 mm; pin smooth-grip ends remain±90 mm. At a nominal axial stop, the ring is16 mm from the cheek and41 mm inside the pin end. These dimensions describe the review geometry only.

Exact solid subtraction finds zero new volume outside each prior collar. The four collars remove0.373 kg using the existing steel density assumption.

CB2 · ATTACHED BOARDING ARRANGEMENT

Steps that stay with the coach

Each regular-coach end now has a hinged stair flight. Fold the handrails down, lift the flight 120° into the end-platform recess, engage both travel pins and close the gate. The eight-bolt removal operation is replaced by retained hinges and separate boarding and travel locks.

Handrails, gate and lift arrangement

Proposed operating sequence

  1. Park and secure the coach. Keep passengers outside the stair and gate sweep.
  2. With the flight supported by the lift, open the gate and withdraw both retained travel pins.
  3. Lower to the boarding stops. Insert both boarding pins and their transverse retainers.
  4. Raise both handrails, insert their upright lock pins and retainers, and check the station-to-first-step height.
  5. For travel, clear the stairs, fold and lock both handrails, support the flight, withdraw the boarding pins, and lift to the travel stops.
  6. Insert both travel pins and retainers; close the gate, engage its slide bolt and secondary keeper. A departure interlock remains to be selected.

No bolts are removed in this proposed routine. Pin access, pinch guarding and lifting controls still require a physical mock-up. The stairs occupy the end-platform recess when stored; they are not an inter-car walkway.

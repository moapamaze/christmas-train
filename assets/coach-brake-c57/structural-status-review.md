# C5.7 structural status

The C56 brake carrier, keyed hub, bearing arrangement, torque link, retained pins and frame brake mounting are unchanged. The independent frozen-geometry comparison verifies all 105 parts supporting the retained C56-D01/D02/D03 sections have identical BREP serializations. The local air assembly moved 50 mm along +X, its base was extended over its unchanged HSS attachment, and its two free hose curves were regenerated. These air-support changes do not revise the brake torque path.

Both existing supplier threads and a broader inbound mail search were checked for this issue. No reply or new controlled drawing was found. Rotor interface discrepancies, exact caliper/pad/chamber matching, physical port assignment and maximum actuator forces therefore remain unresolved. A nominal pressure-area calculation is not substituted for guaranteed maximum service, parking or compounded output.

Earlier bearing, key, hub, pin, weld and frame calculations remain development screens. Their load, material, fit, fatigue and preload limitations are not closed by the hose layout. Final sizing and other brake locations remain pending the requested supplier data.

The revised local support and new body/truck air brackets carry proposed hose/end-fitting reactions only. Their welds, plate thicknesses, engagements and contact paths describe physical geometry; installation loads, local bolster/HSS effects, fatigue, tolerances and field validation remain open. No structural, fabrication or operating release is assigned.

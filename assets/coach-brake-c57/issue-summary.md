# C5.7 — supply connection and service access

The first brake now has two body-to-truck supply hoses with fittings, supported straight leads, sliding guides, mounting hardware, proposed welds and a rounded stand-off guard. The body bracket attaches to the actual C5 bolster. The local air-support assembly is relocated 50 mm along +X; its base is extended over the unchanged HSS attachment and its two free hose curves are regenerated. The truck connection joins those relocated couplings through catalog-sourced Parker 2202P-6-6 elbows. Of 199 C56 parts, 156 retain the same geometry, 40 are relocated and 3 are revised; 80 new parts are added. All 105 primary mounting-section parts retain identical BREP serializations in the independent comparison.

The movement screen combines 45 body postures with 32 suspension/wear postures (1440 discrete configurations). Minimum supply-curve bend radius is 51.83 mm against the 45 mm catalog value; the regenerated local hoses reach 66.68 mm. Revised local hoses are checked anew against all relevant current fixed, primary-moving and body hardware; no earlier C56 hose clearance is inherited. The checked nominal and moving interactions have no positive modeled intersections. These sampled poses do not establish continuous clearance, manufacturing allowance or hose life.

**Hose twist remains unresolved.** Rotation-minimizing frame transport finds up to 21.08° change in geometric end-clock mismatch for the supply hoses and 14.49° for the revised local hoses. This is a compatibility diagnostic, not a measured material twist or stress. Assembly swivels are not live rotary joints. The route needs hose-manufacturer flex validation or further redesign before acceptance; a clear centreline and adequate bend radius do not close this hold.

The straight socket path still hits the existing mount. The proposed 30 mm pull, 60 mm sideways shift and continued pull to 90 mm was checked as six continuous exterior swept volumes, with a minimum modeled gap of 31.35 mm. Full 0–75 mm chamber withdrawal was checked continuously, with 29.94 mm minimum modeled gap. Local complete hoses, guide tops and chamber nuts are removed in the access state; the new supply lines remain installed. Actual caliper cavities, nut seating, socket internals, hand/ratchet space, pad extraction and final parking-release access remain unconfirmed.

No Webb or ZF reply was found in the recorded check. No supplier compatibility, circuit mapping, maximum force or final brake-mount sizing changed. The existing bearing-carrier, keyed-hub, torque-link, retained-pin and frame sections remain linked as C56-D01/D02/D03. Upstream coach controls/trainlines, anti-compounding and additional brake locations remain open. No other brake station was copied.

# C5.7 — body-to-truck hose hardware

Reuse the C5.6 Parker 271-6 / 25-Series hose-end family and STAUFF sliding guides. New source work supports a compact **Parker 2202P-6-6** street elbow where the upstream straight connector would aim into the truck crossbeam.

| Elbow datum | Catalog value | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| M | 24.892 mm | Female axis to male tip |
| N | 13.716 mm | Male axis to female face |
| Flow D | 10.414 mm | Published passage diameter |
| Ports | 3/8–18 NPTF | One female, one male |

The [Parker0700 N42 drawing](https://www.parker.com/content/dam/Parker-com/Literature/Literature-Files/pneumatic/Literature/FRL/0700P/0700P_Tubing_Fittings.pdf) was visually checked. These offsets do not define the full exterior. Body width, thread engagement, clocking and the complete installed envelope remain provisional. **2203P-6 is a tee**, not this elbow. Catalog listing does not confirm current stock.

[Parker’s routing guidance](https://www.parker.com/content/dam/Parker-com/Literature/Polymer-Hose-Division-Europe/Sales-and-Marketing-Bulletins/4400_Rubber-Hydraulic/Technical-Handbook-UK.pdf) calls for adequate slack without snagging, freedom for pressure-related length change, separated planar flex, and protection against rubbing. Consequently, collision-free centreline motion alone does not establish torsion or fatigue suitability under combined truck yaw and body roll. The installed swivel is not credited as a live rotary union.

The [271-6 catalog](https://www.parker.com/content/dam/Parker-com/Literature/Hose-Products-Division/Catalog-4400-PDF-Files/HPD_4400_Catalog.pdf) gives nominal Ø19 mm exterior and 45 mm minimum radius. No exact 271-6/25-Series minimum straight lead was found; modeled lead lengths remain design scenarios. The [319-SA87-H](https://stauff.com/en/products/1130006811) supports sliding hose movement and supplies no axial or torsional anchor credit.

Use the fabricated stand-off protection shown in CAD as proposed geometry. Catalog coil guards have no confirmed installed envelope here. Final hose cut lengths, crimp settings, engagements, pressure flex and application validation remain open. Air-brake product markings do not establish approval for this railway installation. PORT97/PORT116 identification remains physical only; Webb/ZF data still governs compatibility, circuit mapping and forces.

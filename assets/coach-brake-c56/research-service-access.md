# C5.6 service-access research

The service drawings can show the sourced chamber axis, 43 mm studs and the 89 mm rear release projection, together with actual retained custom metal. They cannot supply missing pad shapes, caliper cavities or tool datums.

The PAN17 sequence keeps the chamber installed for pad work. After gaining wheel-side access, it removes the retainer and three springs, retracts the adjuster, slides the caliper toward the wheel for the outer pad, then toward the chamber for the inner pad and pressure plate. The road-vehicle wheel-removal step is not automatically a usable railway procedure. Published AF interfaces are 8 mm adjuster, 17 mm retainer, 24 mm chamber nut and 14 mm guide-pin socket. Use a ring spanner on the adjuster. The illustrations are generic family examples. [WABCO manual, printed pp13-17,33-38]

The chamber GA fixes its rear body at s=240.5 mm and released screw tip at s=329.5 mm from the chamber mounting face. Its 89 mm extension does not include hand or wrench clearance. Existing Ø20 extension and Ø60 x50 tool solids are explicit design reservations; their diameters are not OEM requirements. The Rmax110 stored-release-fixture bound still needs its actual profile and orientation. The global chamber mounting datum remains provisional. [WABCO9253800230 GA, exact0250 row]

A real socket candidate is Stahlwille52-24, article03030024: 24 mm AF, 42 mm length, maximum32 mm OD, 26 mm drive-end OD, 21 mm intermediate bore, and half-inch drive. A full32 mm diameter envelope is conservative for external reach screening. It does not prove internal stud/nut engagement or torque-wrench access. The current nut positions are illustrative, so every installed result retains that dependency. [Stahlwille catalog]

An 8 mm adjuster-tool space candidate is Stahlwille20 8x10, article41040810:180 mm long,13.1/15.8 mm ring ODs,7/9 mm ring thicknesses,21 mm offset,75-degree crank. Exact adjustment-tool equivalence and positioning remain unapproved. [Stahlwille catalog]

Chamber-removal views must disconnect hoses and remove fastening nuts before translating the chamber. TRISTOP instructions prohibit adding washers or reinforcement plates at its attachment and require specified replacement nuts. None of this closes purchased-component matching or structural loads.

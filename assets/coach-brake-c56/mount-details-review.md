# C56 mount details

Three CAD-derived sheets show the retained C55 bearing/hub, spherical link and frame/caliper mount connections. The drawings do not change structural metal or establish a brake-output or mount rating.

- D01 uses true Z=300 sections for the carrier and keyed hub, plus a Z=361.5 section through one split-hub screw. It explicitly identifies zero nominal thrust end clearance, the staged reducer/closed-ring assembly, and unresolved hub datum and fits.
- D02 uses the actual link and true X=350 pin section. It identifies 60 mm grip, 4 mm spacers, washer and cross-drilled positive retention. The separated pin/washer/cotter view explains direction only; it is not a swept service-access check.
- D03 shows the existing welded cross support, its right side-web interface and a true caliper bolt section. Candidate weld locations are annotations on real mating metal. The frame has no modeled bolt attachment. A nominal 30 mm screw through 12 mm plate reaches 18 mm penetration with no tolerance/runout reserve.

Purchased ring envelopes do not expose manufacturer rolling elements, and spherical interiors remain illustrative. OEM thread interiors, exact screw heads and unconfirmed Webb hat geometry are not manufactured details. Final maximum service/parking/combined loads, bearing/fit application, hub split-joint load sharing, pin materials/fatigue, full-thread engagement, supporting plates and weld fatigue remain open.

# C5.5 rotor and PAN17 package evidence

Keep **Webb 55030PK-65 as a conditional development envelope**. Do not release its custom hub or permit automatic substitution with S-33391, CT826 or a 173 mm pilot rotor. The public evidence establishes a replaceable product family and supply routes, but does not yet establish the exact purchased rotor/pad/hub mating section.

## What changed in this revision

The disagreement is now supported by primary manufacturers. Webb's current R32 flyer gives a 6.65 inch pilot and 7.71 inch PCD. Its 2022 catalog gives the same pilot but a 7.72 inch PCD. Haldex, DuraBrake and Meritor publish approximately 173 mm pilots for their cross-referenced CT826 replacements. Those figures must not be blended into a single machining specification.

Webb figures are inch-table conversions, not tolerances. *The 1.11 inch discard value is reported by the official product-page search index and must be checked against the actual rotor marking. The direct page currently exposes JavaScript placeholders.

Meritor's own PB1461 catalog adds a 13 mm flange thickness and a generic sectional datum. Its nominal flange-face to disc-centre distance would be 149 − 34/2 = 132 mm. That is a catalog-section interpretation, not an individual controlled drawing. Its annulus, precise hat profile, thread depth and mating tolerances remain absent. Switching to it would not close the present fit requirement.

This rejects an **unproved interchange for our custom hub**. It does not claim that manufacturer-approved replacements cannot work on their intended original hubs.

## Caliper and pad evidence

The legacy Hendrickson catalog pairs S-33391 with PAN17 calipers S-39094-L/R (WABCO 40175072/73), S-41159-1 / WABCO 232 pads and the legacy 20/24 chamber family. This confirms an OEM application package. It does not confirm the Webb substitution or custom mount.

WABCO's exact 40175072 BOM independently names pad kit 12999806. Its product page provides no pad-outline drawing. Do not equate this kit with S-41159-1, or use shipping-box dimensions as pad dimensions.

The controlled WABCO GA is useful for the caliper interface:

- Reference disc: 330 mm diameter, new thickness 34 mm with −0.2 / +0 mm tolerance, wear limit 28 mm.
- Disc installation datum: 24 ± 1 mm from the carrier mounting face to the nearer friction face. This does not locate the rotor relative to its hub.
- Pad assembly dimension: 34.7 mm with +0.4 / −0 mm tolerance. Approximate rubbing width is 76 mm; this is not the exact selected pad outline or Webb annulus.
- Carrier attachment: six oiled M14 × 1.5 grade 10.9 bolts, at least 18 mm thread engagement, 205–225 N·m tightening torque, numbered order 1–6.
- Maximum transferable connection torque: 10,000 N·m. This is not the actual brake output or a torque cap for combined service and spring force.

No controlled carrier tapped depth or bolt-hole entry chamfer was found. An M14 × 30 bolt through a 12 mm plate provides only 18 mm nominal penetration before tolerances and incomplete threads. Full thread engagement must still be demonstrated.

No source established exact selected-pad swept radii, permissible overhang on the 325.12 mm rotor, or the hot and worn clearance. The Gigant PAN17-1 report contains useful force and annulus data for a different 335 mm / Jurid 539 / Type 24 package; those data are excluded from this design's calculations.

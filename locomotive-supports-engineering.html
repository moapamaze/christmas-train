The 4.58 mm minimum central gap must absorb tolerances, wear and deflection. The initial160×140 cap demanded20.69 mm compression; the revised120×120 cap demands19.70 mm. The 20 mm initial elastomer reservation therefore has only0.30 mm spare and is not accepted. Require at least22 mm usable compression including a provisional2 mm allowance, or revise the support.

DT3 nominal physical-part bounding boxes do not overlap the new support parts. Three hose-corridor bounding boxes were candidates; exact solid checks found no intersection and 159 mm minimum nominal separation from the forward catch beam. Full movement and hose flex remain unaccepted.

Missing covers/retainers, driveshafts, gears, hoses, primary guides, deformation, leading/trailing gear, ladder, tender and couplers require full movement checks. Raised mass needs new CG, stability and wheel-unloading analysis.

A 2700 mm body-pivot spacing at 8° yaw gives a circular chord radius near 9.7m. This does not establish wheel/flange forces or permissible track radius for the 1200 mm truck wheelbase. It is not a track specification.

06 / Assembly, inspection and release

  1. Close interfaces: real masses/CGs, normal/fault loads, spring/damper installation, drive routing, articulation and complete primary-retention details.
  2. Engineer the joints: fits, finishes, fillets, WPS, distortion/NDT, fastener preload/locking and inspection limits. Qualified rail/mechanical review of load paths, fatigue, stability and truck retention is required before construction release.
  3. Fixture and machine: survey body/truck datums; machine seats after the approved weld/stress-relief process; trial-fit bearings/spacers without forced alignment. Confirm lubrication and extraction access.
  4. Install centre and side supports: independently support the body. Set released endplays/gaps; install guided caps and positive keepers. No shell or loose pin serves as temporary body support.
  5. Fit catches: sleeves, lower plates, studs, body windows and heads. Apply approved locking. Gauge full free movement; catches must not carry normal weight.
  6. Fixture tests: accepted combined articulation and primary travel, hoses, edge contacts, spring seating and each retention path. Engineer-defined static/fault proof tests with no occupants, followed by inspection.
  7. Weigh and commission: individual wheel loads, ride heights, operating fluids and crew; controlled track-twist, curving, braking and stop tests under an approved plan. Acceptance loads/limits remain to be specified.

Tools: surveyed fixture, rated hoists/body stands, bore/alignment gauges, calibrated tension equipment, spring/damper rig, wheel scales and qualified weld/NDT inspection. No lift capacity is assigned.

Maintenance: shell bays expose the engine. Support joints are serviced from below with the body independently supported. Pin extraction, bearing-cover access, grease routes and visible catch/side-pad wear must be retained. Final suppliers establish maintenance intervals and wear limits.

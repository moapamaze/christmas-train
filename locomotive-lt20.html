LT20 · POWERED-TRUCK CONNECTIONS · OCTOBER21,2026

Captured pivots.
Defined rebound attachments.

The eight-driver layout retains its current truck positions and low body height. This revision replaces the flat pivot stacks with pocketed spherical joints and adds eight rebound-rod assemblies with removable guide cartridges. These are dimensioned candidates for engineering review.

Inspect the connections

Powered pivot and retention

A longitudinal Ø100 pin holds the spherical inner ring between the body clevis cheeks. The truck carries the outer ring in a capped housing with transverse wings. The pin has an integral head, an anti-rotation abutment and an M80 castellated retaining nut with a split pin. Both housing caps also have positively locked through-bolts.

Rebound locks and guide cartridges

Each axlebox has a Ø24 rebound rod on an outboard clevis,100 mm aft of the axle centre. A shouldered Ø30 lower pin and the upper stop nut both use castellated nuts and split pins. The 20 mm nominal stop gap is a development setting. It is not approved suspension travel.

The replaceable guide has a Ø46/34 sleeve and a 100×56×8 flange. Four M8 bolts retain the cartridge; four M16 bolts attach its 16 mm bracket to the truck sideframe. Drilled heads provide for paired safety wire. The rod guide controls the loose capture rod; the axlebox horns still need their own traction, braking and lateral-guidance design.

What the checks establish

  • 25 sampled assembly positions: 0 rigid intersections,0 full-wheel-envelope intersections and 0 spring-space intersections.
  • At 30 m, the largest sampled bearing misalignment is 3.22°. The retained 6° catalog envelope is a geometric reference, not bearing or operating-radius approval.
  • 10 nominal removal corridors:0 intersections. The pivot pin extracts aft; rod replacement removes the guide bracket as well as the cartridge.

Roll support remains an open design item. The spherical pivots alone do not control body roll. Purple pads show the space for a compliant support, whose stiffness, preload, sliding surface and load sharing must be selected. Pins, brackets, bolts, welds and the modified frame connections also require structural and fatigue review before fabrication.

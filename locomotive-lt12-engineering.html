Power and speed

Working basis:36-inch gauge, three1450mm driver axles, zero grade. Efficiencies are screening assumptions: pump total0.855, motor volumetric0.95, motor torque0.90, chain0.97. A10bar line/valve-loss allowance is added to motor differential pressure. These are not supplier maps, especially at starting/creep conditions.

Tmotor=Δp·D/(2π)·ηt; Taxle=Tmotor·49/21·ηchain; Frim=3·Taxle/0.725. At300bar across each motor:1615.7Nm motor torque,3657.0Nm per axle and15.13kN total rim pull. Three motors share the same differential pressure; their flows add. An ideal equal-flow calculation does not establish anti-spin control.

Q=3·D·nmotor/ηv. Main pump input=(Δpmotor+10)·Q/(600·0.855). Five mph is the development cruise case. Full300bar pull at5mph would require59.3kW including the auxiliary allowance, exceeding the saved55.4kW gross intermittent engine rating. With40kW available to the main pump, full comparison pull is limited to about4.06mph before additional derating. Flow alone limits this ratio to6.81mph;7.5mph is not supported by the current pump-flow assumption. A larger pump cannot create additional engine power.

For the upper-mass steady resistance trial below,5mph needs about27.8kW including auxiliaries. That suggests light-load cruise is plausible; it does not prove starting, curve or continuous cooling performance. At the40kW/full-pull case, total drive loss is about12.6kW: roughly11.7kW hydraulic and0.85kW chain-case heat under these assumptions. Charge-pump, control and auxiliary heat remain additional. The one-fan preference is retained; radiator/CAC/oil-core heat rejection and fan pressure drop are not approved.

At full45cc displacement and310bar pump differential, assumed shaft torque is246.7Nm before auxiliaries. The H1P G4 shaft maximum222Nm is inadequate for that case. A higher-rated shaft/coupling option, configured mounting and engine torsional review are mandatory; no ordering code is released.

Recovered mass cases are54,755kg nominal,59,840kg stress and65,100kg upper screening mass. They are budgets, not weighed completed vehicles. Tender and leading/trailing-wheel weight must not be counted as powered adhesion weight.

Fit, curves and service access

117 new nominal CAD parts are valid.2790 pair samples found no intersections between axle-following drive parts and the saved main rails, crossmembers, engine and pump at−40,0,+50mm vertical positions. The selected internal carrier/guard versus bearing/shaft/motor pairs also have no positive intersection. Intentional joints, chain/tooth engagement representations and all other pairs are outside that test.

Frame top remains1350mm. Nominal input-housing faceY253 leaves only10.8mm to the rail inner face263.8. The chaincase lowest point is443.2mm above rail at nominal height and403.2 at the−40mm sample. These are geometry results, not route or dynamic clearances. No yaw, roll, lateral shift, brake, hose or continuous-motion acceptance exists.

The3100mm driver group still needs a railway curve-negotiation design. Chord sagittas at20/30/50/75m are60.2/40.1/24.0/16.0mm; they do not prescribe axle float or a permitted radius. Rigid horn guides shown here establish a vertical arrangement only. Final steering/lateral compliance, equalization, driver back-to-back/profile and any rod motion must be resolved together.

Two case covers, separate motor and supported-shaft parts are shown for maintenance planning. Exploded offsets are display separation, not proven removal paths. With large wheels and inboard rails, side withdrawal cannot be assumed. Removable carrier members, a supported drop-out/lowering arrangement, lifting points, chain opening/tensioning access and wheelset service sequence must be engineered before the installation is accepted as serviceable.

Control and brake interface

The three parallel motor branches need a system-supplier hydraulic circuit: anti-spin/branch flow control, individual speed sensing, pressure/overspeed limits, bidirectional overrun protection, charge/makeup capacity, case drains, flushing and filtration. Equal flow must not be imposed blindly while wheel rolling radii differ. Engine-speed/torque feedback must destroke the pump under load; a pressure limiter alone cannot prevent engine overload. Hydrostatic retardation is not the independent service or parking brake.

The brake chat receives3657Nm/axle comparison drive torque,376cc motors,2.333ratio,29.44wheel rpm at5mph,117-part new packaging study and the full geometry/movement exclusions. Brake discs/calipers/actuators are not yet present. A final motor/brake-fault and blocked-wheel load case is required; no other-chat acknowledgment is claimed.

Release sequence

  1. Confirm the tightest route curve, rail/profile data, actual loaded vehicle masses and powered-axle load distribution. Resolve lateral motion and the working-rod requirement with the wheel/drivetrain owner.
  2. Have the hydraulic supplier size the complete system and confirm engine continuous power, creep torque, pressure/flow controls and cooling. Obtain motor, chain, bearing, sprocket and coupling application approvals.
  3. Finish and review the carrier/guide/axlebox load paths, axle fatigue, fasteners, retention, spring/damper system, case sealing and supported service-removal sequence. Re-run combined3D movement with the actual brakes and hoses.
  4. Engineer-reviewed bench tests, guarded unloaded wheelset tests, brake/interlock tests, incremental ballast traction/curve tests and thermal endurance tests precede passenger service. No test authorization or acceptance is granted by this document.

Build and maintenance sequence — development only

  1. Obtain configured engine/CAC and hydraulic heat duties, actual core/fan CAD and installed curves. Confirm independent coolant/oil circuits, port directions and bleed/fill/drain requirements before ordering.
  2. Choose a purchased cooling module, fan drive and protective guard. Validate power supply, low-speed cooling, overtemperature response, fan speed sensing and positive maintenance isolation.
  3. Release rail/post loads and mounting pattern with the structural reviewer. Jig the support rails and cassette interfaces; install compliant supplier mounts and positive restraints.
  4. Trial-fit sealed fresh-air and hot-air ducts. Add weather separation, drains, screens and removable sealed joints. Pressure-test leakage as agreed with the cooling supplier; do not feed the cores from the hot engine bay.
  5. Make the split rail joints after weld/strength acceptance. Inspect collar capture, bore fit, fastener seating and rail stiffness; prove each span can be supported and removed from a safe work platform.
  6. Demonstrate fan removal with the actual module isolated and supported: remove the rail span, louvers/carrier and outlet connection; disconnect services, release supplier mounts and withdraw under an approved handling plan. Refit guards and all positive retention before operation.
  7. Commission at the specified ambient and duty: record coolant, CAC and oil temperatures, inlet/discharge air temperatures, fan speed/power and pressure drops, including zero train speed. Check hot-air recirculation under representative wind, fouling allowance and cleaning access.

Fabrication tools depend on released parts: tube-cutting and collar-welding jig, drill jig, sheet brake/rolling tools, alignment gauges and suitable sockets. Handling equipment and acceptance limits follow actual component weights and approved procedures.

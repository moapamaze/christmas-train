Development calculations only. No fabricated section, selected power package, actual hose route, brake capacity or operating limit is accepted here. LT43.1 remains the exterior target; LT42 and earlier locomotive motion results do not validate this new six-vehicle arrangement.
Retained geometry and new proposals
Retained LT43 coordinates: tender pins x = 0 / 5000 mm, z = 550 mm; body x = 400–4600, y = ±1300; deck z = 1100; flat roof top z = 2360; nominal skin 6 mm plus 50 mm lining. The decorative coal cover sits above the roof and does not provide equipment clearance. Truck references are at x = 1200 / 3800.
New proposals: transfer regions centered at x = 650 / 4350 spanning the rail centers y = ±700 and z = 550–950; six fixed interface zones at x = 450 / 4550, y = −550 / 0 / +550, z = 1250. These are placement studies, not fittings, steel sizes or verified clearance volumes. The retained exterior frame also remains unapproved.
Catalog fit calculation
Use the manufacturer's inch values × 25.4: 3098.8 × 1371.6 × 1879.6 mm. The rounded metric values on the source differ slightly. Centering the package leaves 494.6 mm nominal space at each end and 558.2 mm each side before equipment, mounts or service zones.

Frame load transfer
At each end, M = F × e with e = (950 − 550)/1000 = 0.400 m. Each 1 kN of longitudinal force therefore creates 0.400 kN·m of local offset moment that must be transferred into the main rails. This is demand per unit force, not frame strength or a complete load case.
Required later: tractive and compressive drawbar envelopes, emergency braking with unavailable vehicle brakes, buff/draft reversals, coupling side and vertical forces, secondary retention, fatigue, local buckling, torsion, weld/bolt details, bogie load paths, installed equipment shock loads and service/lifting loads. Do not assume equal wheel loads or transfer historical spring approval to the tender.
Attachment-point sensitivity
For each interface, hold the neighboring body fixed and rotate the tender point about its coupling pin with Rz(yaw) Ry(pitch) Rx(roll), then translate it axially. The opposite bulkhead is assumed 450 mm behind its pin, with the retained 800 mm pin-to-pin coupling gap. The tender zone is also 450 mm behind its pin, producing a 1700 mm nominal direct span.
Inputs: yaw −10/0/+10°, pitch −3/0/+3°, roll −3/0/+3°, axial −50/0/+50 mm: 81 samples per zone, 486 attachment samples total. These are independent sensitivities; they do not assert that a train can or will occupy every pose. Coordinates and individual limiting poses are in the calculation JSON.

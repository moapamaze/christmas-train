2. Remove induced twist from the hose design

Parker’s safety guide, §3.8–3.10, requires installation and relative movement without twisting the hose. A numerical allowable twist angle has not been established for this assembly. The C57 candidate is Parker 271-6; fitting names containing “swivel” do not establish a live rotating joint.

3. Replace the assumed compressor drive

The quoted Kubota rear interface is SAE B / 13 tooth and is already allocated to the H1P045 pump. Bendix’s BA-922 universal B input uses 15 teeth, with a 2,000 rpm limit; the engine is rated at 2,600 rpm. Its SAE A / 9-tooth through drive does not establish a main-pump drive path. BA-922 direct installation is rejected. A separate approved reduction drive, lubrication and cooling would be a new engineering package.

Next quote candidate: a complete Dürr E-Bull Rail System. The rail product page publishes 315 Nl/min at 10 bar / 60 Hz and continuous S1 duty. These are unit data, not a guaranteed net delivery after a configured dryer. Request the matched controls, treatment, cooling fans and isolators as one package.

Final capacity is still open. The 140.6 reference L/min default covers service cycling and an assumed 15 L/min leakage allowance only. Add brake-pipe control/recharge, spring-release charging and any repeated release cycling, dryer purge and actual valve consumption. The illustrative net flows above are not OEM guarantees. Convert published normal litres to the same reference pressure and temperature before comparison.

At the default illustrative 250 reference L/min, charging 440 L of tanks from atmospheric pressure to 9 bar takes about 16.9 minutes after the leakage allowance. This is a tank-only ideal estimate; connected lines, spring release, treatment losses and temperature effects can increase it. Agree a cold-start and recovery-time requirement before choosing one or more compressors.

Functional circuit to carry into the installation drawing

Preserve the LT26 automatic-brake intent: a reduction or loss of brake-pipe pressure commands local service application using protected reserves. Feed-pipe failure is a different event and needs pressure protection and a defined stop threshold. A road-trailer positive-control relay kit is not a drop-in replacement for this function.

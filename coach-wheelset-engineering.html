Selected architecture

A conventional press-fitted wheelset is the development basis. Wheel/axle friction must carry combined torque and lateral axial force. The bearing nut reacts against the Ø104 shoulder and cannot substitute for the wheel press fit. Neither wheel rotates independently on this axle.

Geometry retained and changed

C5 wheel hubs remain at Y±480; bearing centres remain Y±620; axle centres X±600, Z300. Wheel hubs span 410–550 and −550–−410; seats span 405–550 and −550–−405. The 140 mm hub controls nominal contact length, not the 145 mm seat. The hub-centre spacing of 960 is not an approved back-to-back or tread contact spacing.

Only unused ends are shortened from Y±1280 to ±665. The axle-2 C5.4 brake keyway and all C5.5 brake parts are retained. CW1 replaces axles, four bearing-nut proxies and four outer covers in the connected-coach composition; the canonical C5 and C5.5 workstreams remain separate.

Bearing retention stack

Positive side, mirrored at the opposite end: shoulder 600; bearing 600–640; spacer 640–643; washer 643–644.75; nut 644.75–660.75; shaft end665; blind-cover inner face675. Nominal end-to-cover clearance10. The 3 mm spacer, Ø86 runout envelope and 10.4×4 washer groove are development allowances, not approved manufacturing details.

SKF 22218 E requires shaft shoulder diameter at least101, housing shoulder opening at most149 and adjacent fillet radius at most2. The current Ø104 shoulder and Ø149 outer abutment opening meet those nominal bounds; tolerance margin and corner details remain open. Bearing rollers/races are simplified CAD proxies.

Both bearing inner rings clamp to shoulders. The left outer ring has ±3 mm nominal axial travel in its housing. Do not loosen the left nut to create float. Housing fits, thermal movement, lateral guidance, grease, seals, deflection and service clearance require application review.

Thread helix, chamfers, tab pattern and bend radius are schematic. Blind covers retain existing holes, but gasket, fastener locking and inner seal design are not qualified.

Press-fit selection still required

No interference number has been selected. A Ø110 nominal callout is not a finished bore/shaft tolerance. The supplier must qualify material strength and fatigue, finite hub/web geometry, surface finish, lubrication, thermal effects, contact pressure, fretting, pressing force/travel acceptance, lateral slip and braking torque together. Wheel and axle material, heat treatment, NDT and traceability are also held.

Release checklist for the wheelset shop

  • Load cases, speed, braking torque, curves and track twist; axle fatigue including the brake keyway and thread transitions.
  • Rail section, turnouts and curves matched to final tread, flange, back-to-back and wheel diameter limits.
  • Wheel hub/web section, measured interference limits, lead-ins, surface finish, press tooling and press-chart acceptance.
  • Bearing fits, internal clearance, permissible misalignment, nut torque, spacer material and face tolerances.
  • Full thread engagement, runout relief, groove-end radii, washer geometry and fatigue allowance.
  • Seals, contamination protection, cover gaskets and full staged assembly/service sequence.

CURRENT PIVOT REVIEW · LT27

Give the pivot room to move.

The review candidate increases nominal slide travel from ±10 to ±14 mm by moving each stop face outward 4 mm. It preserves the 70 mm sleeve, pin, cheeks, traction link and engine installation. Gold stop collars in the model are candidates for engineering review.

What the geometry establishes

Each revised collar occupies a subset of its previous solid. This edit adds no occupied space at unchanged poses. It does not extend the scope of the prior installation clearance checks or clear every arbitrary orientation at the new stops.

What still needs confirmation

The collar is 16 mm long instead of 20 mm. Its bearing-face area remains the same, but its strength and welded connection are unqualified. Actual tolerances, sleeve friction, wear and elastic movement must replace the provisional budget.

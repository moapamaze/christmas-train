DT3 · Motor-to-wheel assembly · R0

The drive behind the wheels

One hydraulic motor per truck drives a central 2.5:1 reducer, two sliding cardan shafts and two 3:1 axle gearboxes. The complete locomotive uses two of these trucks.

Dimensioned mechanism proposal. The model now includes the drive components and their mounting geometry. Gear ratings, final supplier configurations, fits, suspension and brake integration remain unfinished.

LT37 · TANKS AND CONNECTIONS

Fuel and brake air are now in the layout

The previous model allowed weight for fuel and air equipment but did not show the tanks. This installation study adds the tanks, compressor and control-module spaces, connects the new routes to named interfaces, and separates service braking from parking-brake release.

Development installation, with supplier-dependent boundaries still open. The fuel tank is a capacity target. The air tanks are catalog candidates whose quantity has not been sized for this train. The locomotive brake package and the coach’s physical port-to-function mapping remain unconfirmed.

Routing and movement checks

The three front branch gaps have been removed by rebuilding the body junctions. The twelve overlapping lead locations are replaced by longitudinally staggered connections. Each truck now has six separate moving routes: A, B, case drain, service air, parking release and wiring.

Loading the numerical check record…

The 909-pose flex screen combines 504 retained LT33 reference cases and 405 prescribed combinations: straight / 50 m / 30 m, ±2° pitch and roll, −20/0/+30 mm additional route heave, and ±14 mm slide. This checks the declared geometric envelope. It does not establish continuous hose behaviour, twist, pressure expansion, fatigue or approval of a 30 m operating curve.

The cab front panel and liner have two explicit 60 ×70 mm service openings with sealing/hardware holds. Fuel and oil tank centroids retain their prior locations. Brake air hardware and revised route weights still require a reconciled load update before earlier suspension results can be used to accept this installation.

Remaining connections that require a decision

  • Confirm the pump and motor order codes, port positions and charge/flushing/cooling arrangement.
  • Select the locomotive brake equipment and confirm the C57 cast-port identities. The current coach model shows two development brake units, not a validated full brake count.
  • Size the protected air reserves and compressor using actual brake demand, then confirm mounts, fittings, clamps, access and the complete movement sweep.

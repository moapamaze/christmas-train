What the clearance check includes

Every represented moving part is enclosed in a local box, transformed with its truck, axle, spring, slider or link motion. Full Ø1,450 × 103 mm driver-disk boxes cover wheel rotation; the complete 809.244 mm-wide AIE orientation reservation is occupied rather than checking only its outline bars. The new engine-installation boxes are compared with all these moving boxes. Separated conservative boxes establish separation of these occupants at the listed discrete positions.

The 504 poses are not a continuous sweep. This check does not requalify every retained truck/body interface, complete connecting-rod rotation, leading-truck vertical movement, track transitions, hoses, supplier protrusions, elastic deflection or thermal clearances. Spring representations are occupancy envelopes.

Supplier support interface

Danfoss lists orientation-dependent H1P 045/053 mounting-flange moment ratings: 2,020 N·m rated / 4,110 N·m shock with the control on top, and 1,300 / 2,930 N·m with it on the side. These are pump-flange limits, not approval of the AIE adapter, engine housing or proposed skid. The configured mounting arrangement, mass, centre of gravity and support stiffness remain unconfirmed.

Source: Danfoss H1P 045/053 Technical Information, June 2025, printed page 9. Public document checked October 21, 2026. Requested supplier support and torque data remain open; no inquiry is resent by this revision.

Evidence and scope

  • Signed lateral acceleration is v²/50 m for left/right and zero on straight. Six mass allowances and 168 LT25 duty/track combinations are preserved.
  • The quasi-static reduced model assumes rail contact, rigid structure, equal wheel force allocation, fixed LT23 spring preloads and unselected linear bumpers. It is not a dynamic or track-geometry analysis.
  • Capacity flags remain demand-only cases, never authorized performance. No friction-brake capacity or adhesion has been demonstrated.
  • Small-angle load coordinates map to finite rotations without silently correcting a contact position. Mapping residuals, including slight rebound penetration, are reported.
  • Leading truck follows the inherited plan-view path only; no leading-truck suspension or vertical track conformance is modeled.
  • Supplier package and pump are reservations; configured protrusions, hoses, guards, tolerances, elastic deflection and thermal clearances are not represented.
  • No final attachment, coupling, brake circuit, bumper product or fabrication release.

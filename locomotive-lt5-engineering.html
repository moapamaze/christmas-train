Assembly, inspection and service access

  1. Close the structural and OEM holds; issue a controlled drawing set with material grades, tolerances, weld procedures and final fastener/preload specifications. Establish rail-top datumZ0, centrelineY0 and the retained X origin. Do not drill adapter blanks from the envelope models.
  2. Fixture main rails and crossmembers; confirm diagonals, support datums and rail centres before approved tack/weld sequence. Inspect frame welds and HSS local joints under the released inspection plan. Measure distortion before machining/drilling interfaces.
  3. Fit the approved skid sleeve/cap/foot/backing stacks. Gauge grip and stud projection, install the selected locking pairs, establish locator round/relieved fits, and record preload. Demonstrate wrench access from above and below.
  4. Weld and inspect the common engine/pump cradle. Fit supplier-selected captive isolators, then machine/drill replaceable adapter plates from actual OEM geometry. Install the configured package using OEM lifting points; align pump/coupling and verify shaft centre, soft-foot and vibration requirements.
  5. Fit cooler base, bought heat exchangers and completed flexible/sealed duct joints. Pressure/leak-test fluid circuits to supplier procedures. Complete fan guards, rain drainage and hot-surface shielding. Confirm no recirculation with actual fan flow.
  6. Fit rated fuel/hydraulic/battery assemblies with approved restraints and partitions. Complete vents, returns, isolation, drains, abrasion protection and electrical protection from supplier data. Keep exhaust/regen heat away from fuel, wiring, operators and cosmetic panels.
  7. Install sleeved seat/console supports and hard spacers through the finished floor. Fit supplier seats and controls, prove reach/sightlines with an operator mock-up, and check entry/gate/step clearances. Complete panel and restraint hardware only to released patterns.
  8. Trial-fit trim and removable half-panels. Demonstrate filter, dipstick, belt, injector/DPF, radiator-cleaning, battery and pump service access. Establish actual engine extraction/lifting path; exploded display offsets are not lifting instructions.
  9. Commission under a written engineer-approved plan: electrical isolation, start-in-neutral, failed-signal behavior, emergency stop and brake coordination; static/fault load and articulation checks; controlled empty runs; sustained hot-day-equivalent thermal/duty test; leak, crack, alignment and fastener inspections before passenger operation.

Tools: rigid frame fixture, level/laser and diagonal gauges, calibrated bore/thread and height gauges, approved welding/NDT equipment, controlled preload tooling, engine alignment tools, rated lift/stands, electrical test instruments and hydraulic/thermal measurement equipment. Test loads, pass criteria, torque values and service intervals remain to be issued by the responsible engineer/OEM.

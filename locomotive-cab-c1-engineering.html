Cab fit, visibility and open interfaces.

Seat mounting, restraints and mass

The modeled load path is seat / integrated belt → replaceable 500 ×500 ×8 adapter →410 mm high pedestal →500 ×500 ×12 foot and8 mm steel packer →eight total M12-class bolt spaces and compression sleeves →four 100 ×100 ×5 outboard members →four 150 ×150 ×10 rail-side attachment plates. The280 ×286 seat-rail pattern is inherited family data; the exact ordered rail and restraint arrangement must replace it. The floor bolt pattern is 360 square per seat. Steel packers avoid clamping through the finish material.

Only the modeled new mounting steel, at 7850 kg/m³, totals 260.9 kg. This excludes the retained structural floor and is not a full cab weight. Seat, control and finish masses, removed items and actual material assignments must be reconciled into the locomotive mass / CG and all LT32 load cases. No bolt torque, weld size, restraint rating or structural capacity is released. Exact seat adjustment / recline, belt retractor location, release access and pinching remain supplier / mock-up checks.

Underfloor routes avoid the new seat pedestal area. Clips, pipe guards, abrasion / heat protection, water traps, drainage, service removal and full running-gear / step sweeps remain unqualified. Their new endpoints are visibly open; the LT32 first-coach geometry is preserved separately.

The model proposes 15 mm side-lining allowance, a curved headliner25 mm inside the retained roof radius, an 8 mm non-slip floor finish, two shielded task lights, two 240 ×100 fresh-air openings and high rear passive outlets. No additional fan is added. Airflow, demisting, temperature, ingress, lighting levels, night reflections, emergency power, material flammability and actual panel fasteners remain open. The modeled 30 mm front thermal-barrier allowance is not a rated fire wall. The rear cab closure remains undeveloped.

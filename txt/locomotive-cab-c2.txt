What changed in this revision
 | Area
 | Modeled in C2
 | Still to verify
 | Engineer controls
 | Train and locomotive brakes, traction, direction, park, horn, key, emergency stop and camera selector sit at negative Y: the engineer’s left while facing forward. The elevated enclosure clears the seat and knee probes.
 | Final devices, control forces, detents, full arm movement and occupied mock-up.
 | Monitor
 | Brigade 7-inch packaging candidate, forward of the engineer and below the front sill. Independent video-health annunciator space and fault-display prototype.
 | Actual sunlight/night readability, image size, mirror orientation, latency and fault detection. The prototype does not detect hardware faults.
 | Cameras
 | Front camera on a frame-supported mast; rear camera on the last coach’s end-frame bracket. Guards stay behind the lens plane; aim, cleaning and removal spaces shown.
 | Exact OEM mount pattern, aim lock, weather/IR effects, cleaning reach, vibration and a real moving-train test.
 | Wiring
 | Protected separate video/power trunk, capped keyed sockets and flexible jumpers at the locomotive and a repeatable coach joint.
 | Whole-consist cable length, supply loss, shielding, connector count and signal continuity. Camera power is 12 V; OEM interfaces must be preserved.
 | Exits
 | 700 × 1,900 clear openings, raised roof, jamb supports, rear circulation and handholds. Six open treads with seven equal 215.43 mm rises.
 | Inclined-ladder release/lock, broad lowering sweep, ground conditions, grasp and safe descent. Door leaves, seals and latch details are not issued.
 | LT32 reconciliation
 | Net locomotive mass change +383.5 kg. Updated case placements are used by the combined viewer. Cab BP/FP routes now meet the retained rear isolation ports.
 | Remaining supplier, articulation, brake-circuit, strength and capacity approvals.

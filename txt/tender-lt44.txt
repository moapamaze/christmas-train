Carry the pulling and braking loads through the frame
The proposed load path runs from each coupling eye through its drawgear cassette into a deep crosshead, then into both main rails. The two bogie positions stay at 1200 and 3800 mm from the front tender pin for this study. Equipment mounts will follow the selected package drawing.
The 400 mm height difference needs a real structural connection. The orange regions reserve that connection; they do not specify a steel size, weld, bolt pattern or load rating. Body panels and the machinery skid receive no credit for carrying drawbar force.

Give every connection a destination
Use separate supported flex stages at the locomotive–tender and tender–coach gaps, with fixed manifolds on each body. Within the tender, fixed runs should be clamped and accessible; each moving span must be developed against selected hose and connector data.

Keep fuel storage, feed and return within the tender. Keep pump suction short within the machinery package. A/B traction lines and the motor case return cross the front connection; no mechanical driveshaft crosses it. The tender needs its own brake installation, with capacity included in the complete train assessment.
The fixed zone coordinates are proposals at z = 1250 mm, above the 550 mm coupling center. Their final positions depend on moving clearances, access and fitting geometry. They have not passed a hose-routing or collision check.
Movement screening
A first attachment-point screen uses ±10° yaw, ±3° pitch and roll, and ±50 mm axial movement as declared trial inputs. Across the side zones, direct endpoint distance ranges from about 1510 to 1880 mm, compared with 1700 mm straight. This identifies movement the flex arrangement must accommodate; it does not select a hose length or approve these motions.

The standard package does not fit the current roof
The published Hydra-Tech HT150DQV enclosure measures 122 × 54 × 74 inches. In the existing tender, the clear height above the deck is 1204 mm (47.4 inches), so this stock unit projects 676 mm above the inner roof lining. It is a hydraulic skid comparison, not a confirmed all-three power package.
Design direction: retain the present exterior as the target while requesting a custom lower package or a rearranged skid. If the stock enclosure is selected, revise the tender height and recheck vehicle clearance, cooling and center of gravity. Do not freeze frame mounts or roof height yet.
The standard unit also carries much larger tank capacities than our earlier layout. Its published dry mass plus assumed full fuel and reservoir oil is about 3.15 tonnes, before tender structure or additional generator/compressor equipment.

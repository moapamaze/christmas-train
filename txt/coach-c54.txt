Chamber and connection
The controlled WABCO drawing gives the 20/24 exterior, mounting studs, pushrod, two air ports and manual parking-release extension. The model uses the WABCO 9253800250 exterior with the 300° port variant. Catalog left/right labels conflict; the full part number and drawing angle identify this reference. The chamber’s position on the caliper remains provisional.
The drawing specifies 8 bar working pressure and 10 bar temporary maximum. Its information-only force graphs do not establish a certified maximum at those limits. The PAN17 family’s 10 kNm reference is a structural comparison, not proof of this package’s delivered braking torque.
Carrier and axle attachment
The revised carrier separates radial support from thrust location. Needle bearings support the carrier around the axle; the thrust parts and retainers provide the proposed axial load path. The revised hub and key transfer disc torque into the axle.
The short articulated torque link carries brake reaction into the strengthened frame connection. Nominal geometry and gross strength comparisons remain subject to fits, fatigue, local load introduction and the actual maximum actuator load.
Service access is part of the installation
Parking-release access, pad extraction, caliper wear movement and the two chamber hose connections must coexist through suspension travel and truck turning. The commercial caliper and chamber move as the pads and rotor wear; a hose path must allow that motion as well as suspension movement.
The closed one-piece inboard rotor still requires wheelset removal and shop removal / reinstallation of one rail wheel. Pad service and manual parking release are separate access checks. This revision does not claim an on-car rotor exchange or a complete train air circuit.
Engineering and source records
Bearing loads, connection screens, force limits, motion checks and unresolved service interfaces are kept on the engineering page. The remaining brake locations and their air connections follow after this first installation is resolved.

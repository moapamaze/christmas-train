Geometry method
C1 roof outer radius 177.503663″ and centerZ−56.889490″; subtract 2″ hoop depth,1″ continuous insulation and .040″ lining. Headroom at transverseY is Zcenter+√(Rinside²−Y²)−32″. Finished side-wall planes are±(52.125−1−.040)″. No extra furring layer is hidden in these dimensions.
Side, end and curved/raised roof areas are extracted from retained skin solid masses divided by 2680 kg/m³×1/16″. Window area is the 16 clear 30×32″ openings. Door heat-transfer area uses two 32×80″ apertures. Small sheet overlaps, trim and frame edges remain approximate; this is a surface takeoff proxy, not a certified envelope model.

Assembly heat-transfer screen
Mineral-wool resistance is 0.74 m²K/W per inch. Parallel paths combine insulated bays and steel members; a separate 2% /5% direct-bridge sensitivity covers local penetrations. Steel conductivity 45 W/mK and inside/outside surface films are engineering assumptions. Hollow steel is conservatively treated as solid through-depth. Floor and roof inside films use 0.10 m²K/W for all cases, deliberately avoiding a downward-flow film credit in cooling. A 1D model cannot capture lateral heat spreading through steel, so these values remain screening inputs. The higher-loss scenario is not a proven upper bound.
Study frame fractions: 20% walls/ends and 12% roof. Higher-loss fractions: 30% /20%, with higher window U. Door frame fraction 20%;1″ cavity insulation and no continuous layer. Floor study 60% insulated, sensitivity 50%;no thermal resistance credited to the honeycomb, rubber, exposed steel or tray. This keeps the unresolved floor conservative. These fractions are not all measured takeoffs.
The additional 25% floor-insulated case represents substantial underside exclusion. Removing the full-width CI3 body-check intervals over both bogies leaves 23.49% clear; those intervals are study windows, not measured swept envelopes. The 25% case is a rounded sensitivity, not a cleared cassette layout.

Each case uses 37 thermal people at 75 W sensible/45 W latent,420 W internal equipment,0.03 m³/s average door infiltration,1.2 kg/m³ air density and 1.006 kJ/kgK specific heat. Cooling includes 15% allowance. Solar applies 500 W/m² to all clear glazing at SHGC0.5 plus 20K roof sol-air increment; this is a stress assumption, not orientation-specific exposure. Opaque-wall solar and thermal storage are omitted. Humidity stress adds 0.006 kg/kg across ventilation and infiltration. No recovery is credited.
Uwindow 2.7 and SHGC0.5 remain unconfirmed; higher-loss U5.7 is shown separately. The earlier 6 mm polycarbonate alternative does not demonstrateU2.7. Actual glazing, framed opening size, safety performance and mass must be selected together. Comparison against earlierU uses the same updated areas to isolate construction effects; it will differ from archived CL2’s rectangular model.
Winter results subtract occupied sensible gains and show steady-state demand only. Empty preheating, door cycling, moisture, solar storage and heat-pump defrost are not modeled. Thermal BTU/h cannot be used as generator electrical input. No four-coach total is asserted before ADA envelope coordination.

Unresolved construction gates
Check honeycomb spans, concentrated loads, inserts, edge supports and finish/adhesive compatibility. A catalog panel is not an accepted floor design.
Confirm liner clip stiffness, vibration retention, panel span, edges, fasteners, metal isolation and bracket boxing. Wool is not a spacer or fastening substrate.
Integrate 38.75″ regular benches in structural CAD and review seat capacity; retain existing 24″ aisle and feet.
Integrate the separate ADA enclosure, door returns, fold-down seats, wheelchair paths and pin/hand access.
Resolve truck motion, brake routes and service removals before underside trays are placed. No obstruction is accepted in those envelopes.
Complete whole-coach mass/CG, suspension and braking review with the added enclosure budget.
Select glazing, test moisture/air sealing and qualify the assembled transport materials. Individual product fire ratings do not qualify the full vehicle.
Verify ventilation duty, actual fan curves/noise, roof curb/support details, mini-split performance and installed generator output.
Primary product references
Product references establish candidate material properties only. They do not approve rail/mobile installation.
ROCKWOOL Rockboard 40 technical data, April 2026:64 kg/m³; RSI 0.74 per inch;4.3 kPa at 10% compression. E84 indices 0/0 and noncombustibility classification are material data.
ROCKWOOL attachment guide: mechanical retention principle. Building guidance does not provide the vehicle fastener schedule.
ROCKWOOL curved insulation guidance: use staggered layers and retained joints; confirm the actual thin-board roof mock-up.
Plascore standard base panels:AA3.6-80,0.020″ aluminum faces, aluminum honeycomb and epoxy;3/4″ panel 4.6 kg/m². Supplied open edges require closeouts. Coupon properties are not allowable floor spans.
Altro Transflor Motus technical data:2.5 mm,4.17 kg/m²; transport fire-test results apply to the product and stated testing, not the entire assembled floor.
Almexa 5052 aluminum data: density 2680 kg/m³ used for liner/tray mass.

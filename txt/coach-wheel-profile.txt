What the review found
The rail interface is unresolved. The candidate 30 lb rail paper drawing and linked CAD disagree. Finished flange shape and wheel spacing need a matched rail/turnout definition.
The bearing spacer needs a centering solution. Its current bore sits over the thread relief without a positive pilot. The axle-end sheet identifies this for correction.
The inner cover opening needs a seal design. The floating side must accommodate axial travel as well as tolerances and tilt.
The nearest nominal brake clearance is 4.428 mm. This is the current rotor-bolt end to wheel hub; it is a measured CAD gap, not an accepted tolerance or movement margin.

Wheelset-shop review and quote scope
Request one complete first-article wheelset: one axle, two one-piece wheels, approved press assembly and inspection record. Quote the plain axle and keyed brake axle separately. Existing 1330 length and Ø600 running-diameter reference are interface targets, not approved stock or material selections.
Agree the finished profile and wheel back-to-back jointly with the trackwork supplier, using actual rail and turnout drawings.
Return wheel and axle materials, final limit sizes, fits, fillets, keyway details and inspection controls against the hold schedule below.
Confirm the staged assembly of closed brake components before wheel pressing. Retain the current split brake hub and reducers.
Define pressing tooling, lubricant, force–travel acceptance and final wheelset checks. Do not press through bearing rollers or use the bearing nut to install a wheel.
Resolve spacer centering, bearing clearance, seals and nut locking, then review full loads and fatigue before issuing machining drawings.

Assembly and inspection sequence
Confirm the configured package and service-side drawing. Locate the hatch from actual routine tasks before cutting the shell.
Form the leaf and reinforcement to their specified radii. Jig to the retained shell; inspect fit, edge clearance and seal land. Proposed welds require an accepted thin-sheet procedure.
Jig both hinge axes together, assemble pins with new retainers, set the compression dogs and captive end stops, and verify operation. Add and prove a positive hold-open device before service use.
Fit seam doublers and tapped blocks before closing the shell. Verify all removable-panel fasteners can be reached from outside or the intended access opening, with the panel safely supported.
Fit the dome receiver pads and shortened risers in a fixture that preserves the saddle elevation. Engineer acceptance is required before welding receivers to the main frame.
Trial-fit service tools and a representative filter, then demonstrate the final supplier-approved powerpack removal using its rated lifting points and a documented lift plan.
Development tools: sheet-forming equipment, drilling/tapping jig, welding fixture, radius gauges, deburring tools and appropriate sockets. A suitable work platform and rated handling equipment must be selected from actual access height, component weights and the approved removal plan. These are development tasks, not construction authorization.

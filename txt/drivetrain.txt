Motor mount, axlebox and drive
The motor mounting face moves inward127mm, with a shorter geared coupling and revised carrier. New section drawings connect the axle, bearing stack, recessed retainers and motor. The motor moves and tilts with the axle.

What keeps the motor from rotating?
The motor flange bolts to the carrier. Its reaction torque passes through the carrier into the pivoting axlebox cradle and then through two vertically separated guide pads into the truck frame. The fore–aft trunnions allow axle roll while restraining motor rotation about the axle. The coupling transmits shaft torque to the axle; it does not carry the locomotive’s weight.

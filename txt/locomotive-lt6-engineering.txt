Cooling and pump connection
Installed pump power must fit Pcontinuous,derated − Pfan − Palternator − Pauxiliary at the actual duty. The55.4kW catalog gross-intermittent rating is not that value. Pump hydraulic power is p(bar)×Q(L/min)/600; divide by efficiency for shaft demand. An illustrative300bar/130L/min point gives65kW hydraulic before losses and cannot be accepted with this engine. It is not the current selected Danfoss configuration.
Cooling remains a reserved cassette atX5075/5310 with1050×1000 faces, side-panel air openings and a600×900 top outlet. OEM coolant/CAC heat rejection, hydraulic losses, fan/system curves, actual ambient/altitude and duty must set the cores and ducts. Qair=Qheat/(ρcpΔT) is only an air heat balance; it does not size a radiator or prove fan pressure. No cooling-capacity selection was made from the old engine sheet or a guessed heat figure.

Assembly and inspection sequence
Close OEM/structural holds and issue material, tolerance, WPS, locking and preload specifications. Use the retained rail-top datum. Do not machine blank OEM foot areas from catalog envelope boxes.
Fixture the common cradle. Weld proposed gusseted ears and through-sleeves in a controlled sequence; inspect welds, rail straightness, adapter contact/flatness and hole pitch. Provisional cut tolerance±1mm and non-OEM hole location±0.25mm are development targets, not released fits.
Fit pedestal plates and approved captive mounts. Gauge lower fastening engagement and centre-bolt blind depth. Confirm sleeve length, full bearing surfaces and loaded deflection. Install complete engine/pump package with rated lifting equipment at OEM points; align and check coupling/soft-foot by supplier procedure.
Verify each skid clamp’s grip, thread projection, locking, locator fit and wrench access. Apply and record released preload. Verify joint separation/slip response using engineer-approved load cases before running.
Complete coolant/CAC/oil circuits and independent cooler supports from approved thermal data. Inspect flexible connections, fan guards, drainage and exhaust/DPF heat clearances. Demonstrate filters, dipstick, belt, pump and aftertreatment service and extraction paths.
Install selected seats on approved adapters. Verify rail/belt anchor fasteners, full seat adjustment, gate/steps and operator eye/reach positions in a physical mock-up. Finalize console holes only after this test and the brake/traction interfaces are accepted.
Trial-fit door hinges with released pin fits and lubrication. Fit fresh cotters, retained wear pads and positive dog pins. Install the missing hold-open stay before service use. Verify controlled motion, latch engagement, rattle and access without relying on gravity or hand support.
Fit lamp flange studs and frame bracket; demonstrate access to rear nuts and cable grommet. Install arch-supported stack/domes with locked flange fasteners; complete flashings. Remove fittings and panels in sequence and prove reinstallation/alignment.
Commission to an approved test plan: neutral/start and failed-signal checks, coordinated brake/engine stop, vibration survey, thermal duty test, leak/crack inspection and recorded fastener reinspection. Passenger operation requires completed design review and acceptance testing.
Required equipment: fixture and height/diagonal gauges, bore/thread/depth gauges, approved weld/NDT tools, calibrated preload tooling, coupling alignment instruments, rated lifting/stands, full-size cab mock-up, electrical and thermal/hydraulic instruments. Loads, pass criteria, torque and maintenance intervals remain the responsible engineer/OEM’s release items.

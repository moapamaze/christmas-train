What changed
The spacer has a machined pilot. A 2 mm smooth pilot centers a 5 mm stepped-bore spacer. The bearing remains in its original position.
The inner cover moves 12 mm away from the wheel. The main housing and inner outer-ring abutment shorten to make room. The wheel-to-cover nominal axial gap increases from 3 to 15 mm.
The inner opening now has a grease labyrinth. A shaft-mounted flinger sits inside a stationary cup. Both ends use this arrangement; the floating side retains its ±3 mm outer-ring movement.
Retention has a defined stack. Spacer → MB18 locking washer → KM18 nut. The axle remains 1330 mm long, with the nut ending at Y662.75 and the axle at Y665.

Installation sequence
Complete the approved wheel pressing and stage any closed brake components that would otherwise be trapped by the wheels.
Pass the inner cover/cup over the smaller axle end. Install the flinger at its defined position using the approved fit and tooling.
Position the housing and inner outer-ring abutment before fitting the bearing. Keep the floating and locating-side abutments distinct.
Install the bearing against its shoulder with force applied to the inner ring. Verify residual internal clearance.
Fit the piloted spacer, a new MB18 washer and KM18 nut. Engage the inner tab and lock one outer tab under the approved mounting procedure.
Fit the outer abutment and blind cap, seal the static joints, complete the grease inlet and verify free rotation and specified float.
Servicing
Support and unload the bogie first. With the outer cap, nut, washer, spacer and outer abutment removed, release the inner-cover fasteners and withdraw the housing while retaining its loose inner abutment behind the bearing. An approved extractor must grip the bearing inner ring; the housing and rollers must not carry the extraction force.
After the bearing is removed, the flinger and cover can pass over the smaller axle end. Their nominal bores do not require removing the pressed wheel. This establishes component passage, but does not yet qualify the extractor’s access or required force. The exploded view shows component order, not an automatic installation path.

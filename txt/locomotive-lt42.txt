Generator and compressor decision
Mecc Alte ECP3 1L2 and 2L2 are screened out for the full hotel scope. The OEM lists11/13 kVA continuous class H, or10/11.6 kVA class F, at 3600 rpm with 240 V single-phase reconnection. These are possible limited-load candidates only; nonlinear VFD acceptance,120/240 V neutral arrangement and exact order configuration are not established.
A two-bearing alternator drawing exists, but the AIE package has no confirmed spare drive. Its rear SAE B/13-tooth interface is already intended for the traction pump.3600 rpm generation from 2600 rpm requires 1.3846 speed-up; 1800 rpm requires 0.6923. A fixed ratio also requires governed engine speed: 2000 rpm would give 46.15 Hz at that ratio. No pulley, jackshaft, belt pull, torsional coupling or PTO is approved.
The exact E-Bull sheet confirms 44 kg,533×456×221 mm, G3/4 intake andG1/2 outlet. It requires cylinder fans and vibration dampers. Its350 l/min bar e-core delivery at 10 bar/60 Hz is distinct from the generic rail system’s315 N l/min. Neither is a guaranteed configured dry-package output here. The sheet supplies no foot pattern or port coordinates, so those model interfaces remain marked.
Brake and valve decision
Retain the documented PAN17/325×34/WABCO232/20-24 family provisionally. The exact torque/pressure curve and actuator force/stroke data were not obtained. Required regular-coach torque remains 727.65 N·m per brake for the 0.7 m/s² level-track target; actual output and stopping performance are unconfirmed.
A Twiflex MXA alternative has published pressure/force data, but its standard discs are 12.7/25.4/30/40 mm thick and it has no integral spring parking function. It is not a drop-in for the 34 mm rotor. Changing rotor, mounts and parking architecture solely to obtain a curve would add complexity.

Turning, suspension and wear together
The four revised fixed feeds clear the declared combined geometry samples. The audit covers 45 body yaw/tilt states and 32 independent travel/roll/wear states for each axle: 46,080 discrete combinations per bogie placement, including captured maintenance states. There are 40,500 combinations when those maintenance states are excluded. Pairwise state factoring tests the relevant combinations without rebuilding the entire assembly each time.
Failures corrected: the LT41 routes had 28 contact variants involving a wheel, torque-link hardware and moving local hoses. The crossing runs were raised; the rear feeds were separated. No bridge structure was changed.
Less pipe: front physical 116 now joins its existing tail at coach coordinates [1705.274, −80, 700] mm. This removes the downstream extension and three bends.
Checks: both 270-part stations, their mutual interactions and current front/rear body geometry were included. Conservative bounds reduced the check to 895 exact solid intersections; none had positive overlap and no solver exceptions occurred. The nominal inherited-route screen covered 289 polylines and found only the four intended tee locations.
Wear included: caliper/chamber travel of 25.4 mm is included alongside ±7° yaw, 2° resultant body tilt, axle roll and suspension travel. The −60 mm captured-maintenance state is not an operating position.
Hose twist remains unresolved. The 21.084°/14.488° endpoint-clock diagnostics are geometric quantities, not allowable material twist. 84 of 90 supply pose/port cases cannot form a single plane while retaining the current endpoints and tangents. A simple loop sketch therefore cannot establish twist-free operation. Assembly swivels are not credited as live rotary joints.
This is a discrete geometry check, not a continuous sweep or a complete two-bogie vehicle simulation. Spring deformation, tolerances, compliance, actual hose shape, fitting bodies, clamp loads and fatigue remain unqualified. Existing same-station C57 checks are inherited. The revised corridors are ready for component detailing; the moving hose assemblies are not released.

Mass and suspension reconciliation
The old 180 kg combined auxiliary allowance now identifies 44 kg compressor +6.6 kg VFD +3×12.5 kg vessels =88.1 kg at their current model positions.91.9 kg remains unallocated for battery, valves, treatment and associated equipment. It is not demonstrated sufficient. The same catalog vessel is not counted again as a solid steel envelope, and existing fuel/tank allowances remain counted once.
The retained locomotive study mass stays 16,809.9 kg. All 504 retained suspension cases converged after the mass-position update; minimum calculated wheel load is 2.172 kN, maximum jounce 29.322 mm, with no modeled hard-stop force. These are the existing quasi-static model and spring assumptions, not supplier-approved spring ratings. Lateral equipment CG, later fittings/clamps, coach additions and final generator mass still require reconciliation.
Separate 80/150/250 kg generator/drive placement sensitivities atX 4500/6500/8500 mm are recorded. They do not add a selected generator to the model or prove a mounting location.

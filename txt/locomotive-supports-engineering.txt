06 / Assembly, inspection and release
Close interfaces: real masses/CGs, normal/fault loads, spring/damper installation, drive routing, articulation and complete primary-retention details.
Engineer the joints: fits, finishes, fillets, WPS, distortion/NDT, fastener preload/locking and inspection limits. Qualified rail/mechanical review of load paths, fatigue, stability and truck retention is required before construction release.
Fixture and machine: survey body/truck datums; machine seats after the approved weld/stress-relief process; trial-fit bearings/spacers without forced alignment. Confirm lubrication and extraction access.
Install centre and side supports: independently support the body. Set released endplays/gaps; install guided caps and positive keepers. No shell or loose pin serves as temporary body support.
Fit catches: sleeves, lower plates, studs, body windows and heads. Apply approved locking. Gauge full free movement; catches must not carry normal weight.
Fixture tests: accepted combined articulation and primary travel, hoses, edge contacts, spring seating and each retention path. Engineer-defined static/fault proof tests with no occupants, followed by inspection.
Weigh and commission: individual wheel loads, ride heights, operating fluids and crew; controlled track-twist, curving, braking and stop tests under an approved plan. Acceptance loads/limits remain to be specified.
Tools: surveyed fixture, rated hoists/body stands, bore/alignment gauges, calibrated tension equipment, spring/damper rig, wheel scales and qualified weld/NDT inspection. No lift capacity is assigned.
Maintenance: shell bays expose the engine. Support joints are serviced from below with the body independently supported. Pin extraction, bearing-cover access, grease routes and visible catch/side-pad wear must be retained. Final suppliers establish maintenance intervals and wear limits.

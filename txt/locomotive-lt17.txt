LT17 · FRONT POWERED AXLE · OCTOBER 21, 2026
A retained pin with room to come out
The rocker pin connects the sliding spring saddle to the axlebox. This revision replaces its placeholder keepers with a shouldered pin, washer, castellated nut and split-pin lock. A shaped exit relief lets the pin withdraw past the saddle and capture hook.
One connection developed. This detail is installed at both axleboxes of the front powered axle. The LT16 springs, centering arrangement and travel range remain the design basis. Rebound-rod locks and guide-bushing attachments are the next unfinished connections.

What the clearance checks show
The revised parts have no positive solid intersections in the 27 sampled LT16 suspension positions. A continuous solid sweep of the pin’s 87 mm withdrawal step clears the modeled assembly once the nut, washer and split pin are removed. The nominal clearance is 3 mm at the saddle exit relief, 5 mm above the axlebox and 19.5 mm to the guide.
The removal sequence still needs the actual tools, an unloaded-support fixture and a complete extraction path. The relief leaves a 3 mm minimum saddle-deck section locally, so this remains a candidate for structural review.

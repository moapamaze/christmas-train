What changed
Brakes: retained PAN17 /325×34 rotor /WABCO 232 pads /20-24 chamber as the documented standard road family. The custom rail application is not approved.
Controls: removed II93258 from the ready-to-install path. A configured KEf/KRf load-dependent rail-control package needs an exact order code and pressure characteristic.
Power: selected Dürr 1037800300_230_60 plus Invertek ODP-2-32040-1KF4A-MN for development. No traction-pump or charge-flow availability is assumed.
Installation: added two 270-part C57 units by proper 180° rotation, and replaced each plain axle 1 with the keyed geometry. The discarded compressor block is removed.

A regular coach requires 727.65 N·m per brake for the 0.7 m/s² target, equivalent to 195.26 N·m/bar averaged over the reference pressure. Neither a nominal 20 in² chamber area nor a torque factor from another PAN17 rotor/pad/chamber variant establishes this exact package’s output. Actual torque, stopping performance and parking force remain uncalculated until the correct curves arrive.
At an assumed adhesion coefficient 0.05, the coach deceleration ceiling is 0.467 m/s² before unequal wheel loading; the 0.7 m/s² target is unavailable regardless of brake size. A 40%-load sensitivity has only 388 N·m/axle adhesion at μ=0.10, so a fixed loaded brake torque can lock light wheels. Empty mass and adhesion are unknown; use a configured load-dependent control.
The retained train mass is 67,410 kg before unreconciled later equipment changes. A 5 mph stop at the 0.7 m/s² target would be 3.57 m with zero delay or 8.04 m with 2 s delay; these are kinematic examples, not performance claims. If all coach brakes are unavailable, the optimistic locomotive adhesion bound at μ=0.10 is only 0.217 m/s². Grade, fade, response timing and repeated-stop thermal duty remain open.

The drive has 4.0 A nominal output-current margin. It cannot boost a 200 V supply into 230 V. Its full catalog input is 7.97 kVA at 240 V; generator sizing must consider current, harmonics and voltage regulation, not just compressor kW. The old 7 kW other-auxiliary allowance has only 2.69 kW left in this sensitivity, before remaining auxiliaries and treatment.
Dürr publishes 315 Nl/min at 10 bar/60 Hz for the unit. At equal reference conditions, the current service-cycle plus 15 L/min leakage estimate is 100.5 referenceL/min. Normal conditions, dryer/purge losses, parking release, BP refill and actual net dry-system delivery are not confirmed. This is capacity screening, not guaranteed recharge time.
Fit and movement evidence
Nominal exact-solid check: added unit against 812 current truck/original-brake obstacles. The plain axle interfered with the key by 19,416 mm³; the keyed replacement removes that conflict without adding shaft material.
Current front/rear body checks use 89/81 actual obstacles. Both clear at nominal placement.
45 body yaw/tilt cases and 32 axle/heave/roll/wear cases: no positive-volume intersections in the tested separate-axis set. Full combined independent-axle motion and continuous trajectories are not covered.
Rotated hose curves retain sampled minimum radii 51.83 mm supply and 66.68 mm local versus 45 mm catalog minimum. Clock changes 21.084°/14.488° remain unresolved; these diagnostics do not establish permissible material twist. Parker requires installation/movement without induced twist.
E-Bull and P2 catalog boxes have no retained display-mesh surface intersection at nominal placement. This limited screen does not qualify feet, service space, mounting loads or operating vibration.
The added fixed feeds use short outlet elbows and revised corridors around the existing bridge. The first elbow uses catalog M/N dimensions with assumed 8 mm engagement and a provisional 20 mm exterior; actual fitting profile and tool access remain unconfirmed. All four revised branch reservations clear nominal geometry and 45 sampled body poses each; the route comparison finds no unintended overlaps against 289 inherited polylines. Fixed branches still require combined body/axle movement and support checks. See the route records for the rejected straight-outlet attempt and subsequent changes.

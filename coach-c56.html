What remains to be confirmed

  • Purchased package: rotor mounting geometry, matched chamber and caliper, maximum forces and exact fitting engagement.
  • Air routing: Port 97 and Port 116 identify physical stations; their service and parking circuit assignments remain unconfirmed. The local hoses end at truck-mounted connections, ahead of an undeveloped body-to-truck supply loop.
  • Service access: straight chamber-nut socket withdrawal meets the mount. A sideways exit is shown as a candidate; actual caliper-seat, pad and tool clearances remain unconfirmed.
  • Final mounts: bearing fits, hub and key load sharing, pin retention, welds and fatigue remain subject to the confirmed loads and connection checks.

CURRENT LOCOMOTIVE REVIEW · LT29

Catch the truck through a controlled load path.

LT29 compares the rigid collars with a replaceable energy-absorbing restraint and develops the pin, cheek, frame and installation connections. The model shows proposed geometry; no safe or rated emergency catch has yet been demonstrated.

Main breaker check (genset)

The genset is rated 158.3 A (about 38 kW at 240 V, single phase). NEC 445.12(A) allows overcurrent protection up to 115 % of rated current: 1.15 × 158.3 = 182 A. A 175 A breaker complies; 200 A would not. At the 35 kW cap the load is 145.8 A, which uses 0.83 of the 175 A breaker. The basis is the PTI-38's own 175 A output breaker; the fallback is a QOM2175VH in a QOM22225NRB. SELECTED, not tested. Older text saying "175 A (125%)" or "OCPD ≥ 1.15·I" was wrong.

Open items (need a technical ruling)

  • Wet-rail start on the R80 curve (0.88 % effective) without sand: fails by the Stress static-axle method (−0.48 kN) and passes by only +0.74 kN with weight transfer (below the 1.10 margin). Needs a ruling: an operating rule (no wet starts on R80), a lower μ basis, or added adhesion.
  • Wet reverse starts without sand are only possible on the level (limit ≈ 0.2 % with the 1.10 margin).
  • Rail basis: the PE package uses 26 kN, but the K2-a driver-wheel target at the pull limits is 26.5 kN (static limit 27.0 kN). At 27 kN the 60 lb rail is 106 MPa, still well under 172 MPa; the basis should be restated.
  • S1-end lug count (2 per regular coach, 4 on C4 = 10 per train) and its Z position are taken from the S1 ruling and the replaced saddle; confirm.
  • Loco 20,406.36 kg is pending freeze.

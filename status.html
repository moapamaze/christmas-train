Main breaker check (genset)

The genset is rated 158.3 A (about 38 kW at 240 V, single phase). NEC 445.12(A) allows overcurrent protection up to 115 % of rated current: 1.15 × 158.3 = 182 A. A 175 A breaker complies; 200 A would not. At the 35 kW cap the load is 145.8 A, which uses 0.83 of the 175 A breaker. The basis is the PTI-38's own 175 A output breaker; the fallback is a QOM2175VH in a QOM22225NRB. SELECTED, not tested. Older text saying "175 A (125%)" or "OCPD ≥ 1.15·I" was wrong.

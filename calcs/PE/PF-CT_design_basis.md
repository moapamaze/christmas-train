# PF-CT Design Basis — rev L4.2

## 1 Scope
36 in (914.4 mm) gauge amusement train for Pulsipher Farms, Logandale NV: steam-outline diesel-hydraulic locomotive (8 × Ø1,450 spoked drivers on two inside-frame powered trucks + Ø650 leading truck), tender T2 carrying the traction engine, hydraulic pump and coach generator, and 4 enclosed coaches (3 regular + 1 accessible) with emergency stairs both sides.

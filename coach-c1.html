J01 preliminary assembly sequence

  1. Survey actual tube outside widths and corner profiles. Trial-fit the cap and connection on an unoccupied prototype; four end-post receivers require a separate design.
  2. Cut the proposed web/backing/shim/rib blanks. Machine sleeves and jig-bore aligned holes through both HSS walls. No sleeve press fit or bolt tightening value is released.
  3. Trial-fit the sleeve/backing/web stack. Interior bracket nominally clears adjacent crossmembers by 0.5 in; check actual welds, tools, stock tolerance and access.
  4. Develop and qualify the proposed rib/web weld sequence and sleeve retention, including distortion, fatigue and inspection requirements. Verify cap/sill contact and effective end welds against actual corners.
  5. Check panel notches and sealed removable closeouts before installing floor or skin. Record actual hardware stack, wrench access, fit and inspection results before coating.

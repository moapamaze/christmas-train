Proposed assembly and inspection sequence

  1. Approve DT drilling/weld interfaces and released machining drawings first. Verify certified pin stock, bearing identity, bolt/washer grades and measured dimensions. No welding of heat-treated pins.
  2. Fixture and weld approved adapters, foot brackets and body supports; inspect welds and machine critical bearing/attachment datums after distortion is measured. Seats must be coaxial and square to the accepted drawing tolerances.
  3. Deburr and flush intersecting grease drillings. Install yaw sleeve/thrust washer and flanged pin; qualify countersunk head seating, usable thread engagement and threadlocker cure. Record accepted preload method and witness marks.
  4. Install complete spherical bearings using supplier tooling on the ring being fitted. Measure actual outer-ring widths; fit shoulder/cover lip shims without unwanted ring preload. Measure inner spacer stacks before tightening keepers.
  5. Assemble headed roll and pitch pins, spacers, keeper plates, wedge-lock pairs and antirotation screws. Establish fastener preload from the final joint calculation; no generic torque value is assigned here. Confirm grease flow and tool access.
  6. Install side-support wear/backing/heel brackets and adjusted datum packers. Verify unloaded gap, guide rebound gap and actual supplier rubber compression. Fit catch studs, approved slotted nuts and new cotters; inspect head/slot overlap throughout motion.
  7. Fit floor beams, ladders and removable shell saddles using approved weld/bolt details. Trial-remove panels with lifting provisions; demonstrate engine and pump service access using actual OEM equipment.
  8. Before passenger service: engineer-approved static/fault proof, articulation and twist test, brake tests, controlled low-speed runs, thermal/duty testing and post-test crack/fastener inspection. Test loads and acceptance criteria remain to be issued.

Tools: rigid machining/inspection fixture, calibrated micrometers and bore gauges, thread gauges, surface/height measurement, bearing installation tooling, controlled preload tooling, grease-gun coupler clearance gauge, approved welding equipment and rated handling/lifting fixtures. Maintenance must expose each keeper, cover, grease nipple, wear pad and catch cotter without removing the diesel. Intervals and wear limits await supplier and engineering approval.

Both released clearance and the stock shoe remain unresolved

The current nominal wheel diameter is 600 mm, with an 80 mm usable tread band in the unfinished wheel geometry. The identified 24-inch / 609.6 mm commercial shoes are at least 84.074 mm wide where width is documented. That exceeds the available band before lateral clearance is allowed.

The 70 mm wide reference insert also fails released clearance under combined suspension probes; additional retraction has not resolved the flange interference. The mechanism retains a replaceable head interface, but it needs a different release / tracking solution or a compatible alternative architecture. No shoe trimming, assumed bedding correction or silent wheel change closes these failures. Shoe curvature, width, attachment, friction material and heat duty require supplier confirmation; the inboard-disc alternative remains available if a compatible shoe cannot be secured.

Connections shown

The model contains fabricated beam, hanger, head and support parts, with spherical-joint and retention candidates. Remaining nominal intersections mean the mating parts and retention details are not a verified fitted assembly. It is a one-axle module, not a complete connected brake. The MGM clevis and thread engagement, spherical locator end joints, and centering / return springs remain unmodeled or unresolved. Their exact dimensions and the failed released-motion results belong to the drawing and geometry records.

Wear adjustment and suspension tracking are separate. Nominal joint movement or a zero-intersection check does not establish friction contact, fatigue life or freedom from binding through the full operating envelope.
